OMR Mobile React v4.7 - AUTO SCAN / AUTO GRADE

- Camera preview analyzes the sheet continuously.
- Auto-grade is ON by default.
- Requires: 4 main markers, >=4 auxiliary markers, valid exam code, low motion, same recognition result for 3 consecutive frames.
- When stable 3/3, app automatically captures a high-resolution still and runs the final grade.
- Manual "Capture & grade now" remains as fallback.
- Auto-grade pauses after a successful final grade to prevent duplicate grading of the same sheet.
- Use "Quét bài tiếp theo" to re-arm for the next sheet.
